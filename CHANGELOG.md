- evidence-weighted confidence updates
- recent trend tracking

- exercise mastery tracking
- adaptive daily workout generation
- transfer-oriented boss rounds
- self-assessment blended with objective evidence

- vocabulary diversity
- repeated-word detection

- prompt/constraint hit rate

- flow lab
